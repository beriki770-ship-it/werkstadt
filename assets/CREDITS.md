Every asset Werkstadt ships is CC0 or the author's own work. Nothing here needs
attribution to be legal; it is credited because provenance is worth more than
the licence requires, and because a reader who wants a different bus should be
able to find where this one came from.

  credit or attribution when using them (although it is appreciated)"*, *"You

  redistribution is explicitly allowed. Textures, HDRIs and the photogrammetry

- **Kenney** — the licence field on each asset page, e.g.

  third-party asset: it is Wild Digital Moments' own model, released under this
  repository's licence.

**What is NOT here, and why.** An earlier build of this world used 35 models
generated on a hosted Tencent Hunyuan 3D account. The open-weights licence that
would have covered their outputs excludes the EU in its own first sentence, and
the hosted service's terms could not be retrieved at all, so no clause grants
redistribution. They are not in this repository and never were --
`assets/fetch_assets.py` rebuilds the same manifest keys from the CC0 packs
below. The reasoning is in docs/PUBLISH-RESEARCH.md, section 4.

vendor model, flattened and joined into a SINGLE primitive by
`npx @gltf-transform/cli optimize` and compressed with meshopt -- life.js
draws only the first mesh it finds, so the join is not cosmetic.

`model.hy.bicycle` and `model.hy.drone` are in this table too: the bicycle
is the Quaternius Public Transport model re-packed, and the drone is Wild
Digital Moments' own ORNIS airframe, released under this repository's
licence rather than CC0.

**Four subjects and the thirteen landmark buildings have no model, and
are left empty on purpose** -- no CC0 equivalent exists in any pack
checked: `model.hy.parrot`, `model.hy.chicken`, `model.hy.busstop`,
`model.hy.playground`, and every `model.hy.lm_*`. life.js and globe.js
skip a key they have no model for and fall back to their procedural
forms -- a drawn parrot, a procedural shed for a landmark, nothing at
all for a hen.

`model.hy.bus` is a BOX LORRY (Kenney `truck.glb`): no CC0 pack checked
ships a bus, and Quaternius's is an untextured grey shell. The manifest
key keeps its name because life.js reads it to decide which streets carry
a large vehicle.
